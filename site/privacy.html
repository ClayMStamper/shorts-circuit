Privacy Policy

Effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · Operated by {{OPERATOR_NAME}}

Shorts Circuit is a desktop application for Windows that helps a creator curate their own short videos and publish them to their own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn and Snapchat. It runs on the creator's computer. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and how to remove it.

Who uses the app

The app is used by the creator who installs it, to publish to accounts they own or manage. It does not provide a public service and does not collect information about other people.

Information the app handles

  • Your videos and notes. Video files you import, the edits you make, captions, hashtags, tags, ratings and permission notes. These stay on your computer.
  • Connected account details. When you connect an account, the app receives an access token from the platform and basic account details: the account or page name and its platform identifier (for example your Instagram professional account id, your YouTube channel id, or your LinkedIn page id).
  • Publishing results. Identifiers and links of the posts the app publishes for you, their processing status, and, where the platform provides them, simple counts such as views, likes and comments.
  • TikTok post list (optional). If you turn on new-post detection, the app reads your own TikTok profile name and the titles, captions, cover images and links of your public posts, to notice new posts. It does not download your videos from TikTok.

How the information is used

Only to do what you ask: show your library, prepare each publication, upload the video you approved to the account you chose, follow its processing, and show you the result. The app does not use your information for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with anyone other than the platform you are publishing to.

Where it is stored

  • Your library, publication history and settings are stored in the app's data folder on your computer (on Windows, under %LOCALAPPDATA%).
  • Access tokens are stored in your operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager), never in the app's database or logs.
  • The app talks directly from your computer to the platforms' official APIs (Google/YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, Snapchat, TikTok). Their own privacy policies apply to what you publish there.
  • If you use the optional sign-in helper for TikTok (an "OAuth broker" you run yourself), it only relays sign-in codes and tokens to your computer and keeps nothing after a sign-in completes. {{OPERATOR_NAME}} does not operate a server that receives your data.

Platform data

Data received from YouTube API Services, the Instagram Platform, LinkedIn's APIs, Snapchat's APIs and TikTok's APIs is used only for the purposes described above and is handled according to each platform's developer terms. Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Removing your information

See Data deletion. In short: disconnect accounts in the app's Settings, revoke access in each platform's settings, and delete the app's data folder.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is meant for creators managing their own accounts.
