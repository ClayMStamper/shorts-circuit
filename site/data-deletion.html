Shorts Circuit

Data deletion instructions

Effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · Operated by {{OPERATOR_NAME}}

Shorts Circuit keeps your information on your own computer; {{OPERATOR_NAME}} does not hold a copy on any server. To delete everything the app has about you and your accounts:

  1. Disconnect your accounts in the app. Settings → for each YouTube channel, Instagram account, LinkedIn page and Snapchat profile, choose Disconnect. This deletes the stored access tokens from Windows Credential Manager.
  2. Revoke the app's access on each platform.
    • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions (or Apps and websites) → remove the app.
    • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove the app.
    • LinkedIn: Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services → remove the app.
    • Snapchat: Settings → Connected Apps (or accounts.snapchat.com) → remove the app.
    • TikTok (if you used new-post detection): Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions → remove the app.
  3. Delete the app's data. Uninstall Shorts Circuit, then delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\io.github.claymstamper.shorts-circuit (library database, imported originals, renders and export packages). Export folders you chose yourself are not removed automatically; delete them too if you like.

When you remove the app in Instagram's settings, Meta notifies the app's owner of a deletion request. Because no copy of your data is kept anywhere but your computer, the steps above complete the deletion. For help, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
